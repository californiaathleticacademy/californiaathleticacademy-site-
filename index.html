// Proven CIF Southern Section Staff

Beginner to varsity. One real pathway.

We don't sell illusions — we cannot turn anyone into a star overnight. Real development takes hard work. But our staff deeply understands the exact step-by-step process required to move a player forward.

Stage One

Beginner

For the true beginner learning the game — fundamentals, footwork, and a real introduction to half-court spacing.

Stage Two

Intermediate

For the player leveling up — sharper decision-making in ball movement, screen actions, and team defensive concepts.

Stage Three

Advanced

For the athlete preparing for the varsity spotlight — full-speed half-court reps against real CIF-level instruction.
